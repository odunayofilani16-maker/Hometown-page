Visit Owo

Discover ancient Yoruba heritage and royal history in this historic town at the heart of Ondo State.

Top three Activities to do at Owo

Tour the olowo's palace

The Olowo's Palace is a remarkable place to step into centuries of Yoruba royal history.

Visit the Owo Museum of Antiquities

The Owo Museum of Antiquities is a great place to see the town's artistic heritage up close.

Explore the local market and try the food

Owo's local market is a lively place to sample authentic Yoruba cuisine and craftsmanship.
